WHAT YOU ARE INSTALLING
- Delft3D 4 computational engines (Delft3D-FLOW, WAVE, D-WAQ, PART), compiled unmodified
  from the official open-source code: https://github.com/Deltares/Delft3D
  Release DIMRset_2026.02, commit 5a4649830b1e5072caf019fb4850bbdefd9ad431.
- Delft3D 4 Community Launcher: a small window to run your models (community code).
- Intel Fortran/oneMKL/MPI and Microsoft Visual C++ runtime libraries the engines need.
